Passvault

Connect your server

Passvault is self-hosted — this extension only ever talks to the instance you point it at, and asks your browser to confirm access to that one origin. It never sees any other site's data.

  1. 1Enter your Passvault instance's full URL, starting with https://.
  2. 2Chrome will ask you to confirm access to that origin — accept it.
  3. 3Click the Passvault icon in your toolbar and log in with your master password.
Free, open-source, zero-knowledge — github.com/toshost/passvault
